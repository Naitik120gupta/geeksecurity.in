If it just happened

Lost money to a fraud? The first hour decides.

Banks can freeze the money only while it is still sitting in an account. Every hour you wait, it can move again.

  1. Call 1930. The national cyber fraud helpline alerts the banks your money moved through.
  2. Call your bank. Block the card and UPI.
  3. File at cybercrime.gov.in with screenshots, the UTR number and the scammer's UPI ID.
